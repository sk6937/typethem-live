03 FAQ

How do I copy a character from this page?

Search or browse to what you need, then tap its key — it is copied to your clipboard instantly. Paste it anywhere with Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac). Everything here is free and needs no sign-in.

Do these characters work on phones and in social media?

Yes. These are standard Unicode characters, so they paste into almost any app, browser, document, username or bio. A few older or plain-text-only fields strip them out — that is a limit of that field, not the character.

Why do emoji look different on iPhone, Android and Windows?

Each emoji is one Unicode character, but Apple, Google, Microsoft and Samsung each draw their own artwork for it. The meaning is identical everywhere — only the picture changes.
